# 2026-11-25: No images taken from other websites

**Decision:** We do not use images copied from other websites in landing pages or ads.

**Context:** The founder suggested using images from other websites so the brand looks professional.

**Choice / why:** CEO declined:
- Copyright infringement.
- Misleading advertising (shows a product/results that are not ours): CCPA guidelines, ASCI code, Meta ad policies.
- Risks the Meta ad account being restricted.

**What we use instead**
- Our own product photos.
- Licensed free stock for mood shots only (never presented as our product or results).
- Original illustrations, labelled "illustrative".

The founder's goal (a professional look) is being met through the Mrudu brand kit (Marketing Lead, in progress: `ventures/mrudu-shower-softener/marketing/brand/`).

**Open risks:** stock licences must be recorded per image when used.

**Approved by:** CEO (Chanakya); founder's suggestion declined with reasons.
