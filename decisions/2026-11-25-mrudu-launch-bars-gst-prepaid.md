# 2026-11-25: Mrudu launch bars: GST-inclusive, prepaid only

**Source:** Finance Analyst, section 0 of `ventures/mrudu-shower-softener/finance/unit-economics.md` (reviewed by CEO). Updates the Mrudu bars in [finance results](2026-11-25-finance-results.md); that file is not rewritten.

**Decision:** Mrudu is priced at **₹1,999 including GST**, **prepaid only** (no COD). These are the bars for the smoke test:

| Bar | CPO (₹ per paid pre-order, net of refunds) |
|---|---|
| Max CAC (KILL above) | ₹730 |
| GO (70%) | ₹511 |
| Trusted GO at ~25 orders | ₹475 |
| Trusted KILL at ~25 orders | ₹1,096 |

- The old placeholder CPO of **₹800** (CPC ₹12, CVR 1.5%) is now **KILL** (was ITERATE).
- **₹25k for 25 orders = ₹1,000 CPO**, which is KILL territory. A trusted GO needs **≥53 orders from ₹25k**.
- A result between the trusted bars needs an extension to about **50 orders**, costing about **₹36–50k**.

**Why:** MRP rules require tax-inclusive prices; prepaid avoids the 20–30% COD non-delivery risk flagged earlier.

**Open risks**
- All inputs are assumptions (landed cost ₹600 needs a supplier quote; GST 18% / HSN 8421 21 to confirm with a CA).
- Bars assume GST on Meta spend is reclaimed (GSTIN on the ad account). If not, finance gives max ₹619, GO ₹433.
