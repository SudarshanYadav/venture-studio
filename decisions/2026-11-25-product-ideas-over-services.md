# 2026-11-25: Pursue product ideas over services

**Decision:** Pursue physical D2C product ideas rather than a services business.

**Context:** The founder proposed two product ideas: a clean-label **electrolyte brand (India-wide)** and a **shower-filter D2C brand (Bangalore)**. Budget $10k (~₹8.5L), goal steady side income, proof of concept in 3–6 months.

**Choice:** The CEO favoured the **shower softener (Mrudu)** as the lead venture: lower capital needed, a clear differentiator (hardness reduction, not chlorine, proven with a test strip), and it can be tested locally in Bangalore. The **electrolyte mix (Lavan)** is kept as the second test; it is a crowded market with tight economics and needs FSSAI compliance.

**Approved by:** founder (direction), CEO (recommendation).
