# 2026-11-25: Mrudu cartridge capacity is unmeasured; ad spend blocked

**Decision:** No Mrudu ad spend until cartridge softening capacity is measured. Bench test proposed; **founder said "Not yet"** (2026-11-25). Status: **BLOCKER, open.**

**Context**
- Finance estimated a resin shower cartridge holds about **0.2–0.4 L of resin**, so it would soften only about **20–60 L of 300–600 ppm water**, which is about **one shower**. This is an order-of-magnitude estimate, **not measured**.
- Marketing Lead and Developer independently reached the same view: no ads should run until capacity is measured (see `ventures/mrudu-shower-softener/marketing/test-plan.md`, `ventures/mrudu-shower-softener/landing/README.md`).
- If the estimate holds, the core promise (hardness reduction, proven with a test strip) fails after the first shower, and the ₹599 cartridge model does not work.

**Choice / why**
- Proposed bench test: about **₹3–5k**: buy 2–3 resin shower units and test strips, measure ppm against litres passed. Cheapest way to avoid spending ~₹25k on ads for a product that may not work.
- Until measured, cartridge life is shown as **`[TO BE MEASURED]`** everywhere (landing, creatives, BRIEF). No "2–3 months" or litres claim anywhere.

**Open risks**
- Founder has not approved the bench test, so all Mrudu spend is blocked with no date to unblock.
- If capacity is ~1 shower, the venture may need a different product form (e.g. larger inline/whole-bathroom unit) or a KILL.

**Approved by:** CEO (Chanakya) recommended the test; founder declined for now ("Not yet", 2026-11-25).
