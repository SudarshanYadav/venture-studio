# Market sizing

Studio wrapper (written by the studio recruiter, 2026-11-26). The method itself is in [references/market-sizing.md](references/market-sizing.md), taken unchanged from coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT, see `../LICENSE-marketingskills`). Read that file before sizing any market.

Studio adaptations:
- Our unit is a physical consumer product sold in India or the UAE, not SaaS seats. Bottom-up = reachable households/buyers x price x repeat purchases x realistic capture; channel-led = ad budget / expected CPO (use the finance model's max CAC).
- Size SOM against the founder's goal (steady side income, $10k budget), not against venture-scale TAM.
- Run at least two methods and show the range, assumptions, confidence (High/Medium/Low/Unknown) and the one assumption that would change the answer.
- Examples in the reference are SaaS and illustrative only; do not reuse their numbers.
