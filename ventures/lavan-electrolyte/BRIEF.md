# Lavan: clean-label electrolyte mix (India-wide)

Status: smoke-test planning · Priority: 2 (second test) · Brand name **Lavan is a placeholder** pending trademark check.

## Product
- Clean-label electrolyte drink mix (no artificial colours/sweeteners), **20 sachets for ₹599**.
- India-wide D2C, shipped by courier.
- Audience: active people, outdoor workers, hot-weather daily hydration.

## Rules
- **Never call it "ORS"** or imply it treats dehydration/diarrhoea; ORS is a regulated term.
- **FSSAI**: licence/registration and compliant labelling before selling (a pre-order test must say it ships after FSSAI compliance, or use an FSSAI-licensed co-packer).
- No medical or performance claims beyond what the label and law allow. No fake reviews.
- Honest pre-order and refund terms.

## Market and economics
- **Crowded market** (many funded electrolyte brands); differentiation must be real (clean label, price per serving, taste).
- **Tight economics**: profit per order ≈ ₹269; max CAC ≈ ₹437 (counting 25% of repeat); **GO if CPO ≤ ≈ ₹306**. At placeholder CPC/CVR the verdict is KILL.
- GST-inclusive pricing cuts max CAC to ≈ ₹289.
