# Mrudu: ad creatives (4 angles)

All copy passed the checklist in `test-plan.md` §9. Placeholders in [brackets] are filled **only** from our own measured test or the live landing page; if a placeholder can't be filled, drop that line. Brand name is a placeholder.

**Shooting rules (all angles):** real photos/video we shoot ourselves on a phone, real Bangalore borewell water, real unit. No stock images, no AI-generated "results", no people's skin or hair, no body before/after. Film each demo in one continuous take, keep the raw files in `marketing/evidence/` with date and location. Burn-in captions (most views are sound-off). Deliver each ad as **4:5 (1080×1350)** and **9:16 (1080×1920)**, video 12–20s, hook in the first 2 seconds, product + price card in the last 3 seconds. Primary text: key point in the first 125 characters.

**Cartridge-capacity dependency (CEO update):** Finance estimates a cartridge may soften only ~20–60 L of borewell water. Every angle says Mrudu "reduces hardness", so **all four depend on capacity being measured and adequate**; A (strip) and C (lather) depend most, since a fresh-cartridge demo would mislead if softening stops within one shower. No copy may state or imply lasting softening or a cartridge life in months. Proof numbers stay `[TO BE MEASURED]`. If capacity is low, none of these ads can run.

## B. Hardness, not chlorine
**Hypothesis:** an "I bought the wrong filter" insight converts people who already own or considered a chlorine filter. Read: B's CTR and CPO vs A.

- **PT1:** Many shower filters are built to remove chlorine. Borewell water in Bangalore is often a different problem: hardness, from calcium and magnesium. Mrudu uses ion-exchange resin, the method home water softeners use, to reduce hardness. ₹1,999 incl. all taxes and first cartridge. Refundable pre-order.
- **PT2:** Chlorine filter or hardness softener? They do different jobs. White crust on the shower head and soap that barely lathers point to hard water. Mrudu targets hardness. Bangalore only. ₹1,999 incl. all taxes; replacement cartridge ₹599. Refundable pre-order, ships by [SHIP DATE].
- **HL1:** Built for hardness, not chlorine
- **HL2:** For hard borewell water in Bangalore

**Visual (static, 4:5):** two shower filters on a plain bathroom shelf, labels facing camera: a generic chlorine filter (brand covered or out of focus, no competitor logo) tagged "Chlorine", Mrudu tagged "Hardness". Optional 10s video: hand points at each, then a strip test on Mrudu only. Do not show a competitor's brand or claim it "doesn't work".

## D. Scale on fittings
**Hypothesis:** naming a visible household problem (crust on taps/heads) reaches people who never thought about "hardness". Read: D's CTR vs A–C.

- **PT1:** That white crust on taps and shower heads is limescale: calcium and magnesium left behind by hard water. Mrudu reduces hardness at the shower, before water reaches the head. Check it with a test strip. ₹1,999 incl. all taxes and first cartridge; replacement cartridge ₹599.
- **PT2:** Hard borewell water leaves white scale on shower heads and glass. The cause is hardness. Mrudu is a resin shower softener that fits a standard shower arm and reduces hardness, which you can check with a strip. ₹1,999 incl. all taxes. Refundable pre-order, ships by [SHIP DATE].
- **HL1:** White crust on the shower head?
- **HL2:** Tackle hardness at the shower

**Visual (static + 10s video):** macro shot of a real crusted shower head and tap in a Bangalore flat (ours or with written owner permission; no people, no identifying details). Video: slow pan across the crust → cut to Mrudu fitted on the arm → strip test result. Do not show a "cleaned" shower head as a result; we haven't tested scale reduction.
