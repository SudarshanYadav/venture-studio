# Offline test: test-strip demo at a Bangalore apartment (RWA)

**Blocked until** the cartridge-capacity test (`test-plan.md` §0) shows softening over a realistic volume. Do not demo with a fresh cartridge or mention cartridge life.

**Goal:** cheap read on whether people pay ₹1,999 after seeing their own water tested, plus the words they use. Run alongside the Meta test; orders tracked separately (never counted in Meta CPO).

**Where:** one gated apartment on borewell/tanker water (e.g. Whitefield, Sarjapur Rd, Electronic City). Weekend morning, 2–3 hours, a table near the entrance or clubhouse. Requires RWA permission (**contacting the RWA = founder OK**).

**Cost (≈ ₹1,500–2,500):** hardness strips (pack of 50–100) ₹600–1,000 · two A4 signs + printed QR to landing page ₹200 · demo unit (same one used for the shoot) · bucket/glasses ₹200 · RWA fee if any (ask first).

**Pitch (30 seconds):**
"Hi, we're testing a shower softener for borewell water. Want your tap water tested? It takes 30 seconds. [dip strip] This colour is the hardness: about [reading] ppm. Here's a strip from water through our filter after [TO BE MEASURED] litres: [TO BE MEASURED] ppm. It's ₹1,999 including taxes and the first cartridge; cartridges are ₹599. It's a pre-order: we ship by [SHIP DATE], and you can get a full refund any time before it ships. If you'd like one, the QR goes to the order page."
Do not say anything about hair, skin or health. If asked, say: "We only claim what the strip shows: lower hardness."

**Rules:** test only water people bring or a common tap, with permission. No photos of people. Quotes may be used in ads only with written consent. Payment only through the landing page (QR with `utm_source=rwa`); no cash.

**Record (tally sheet, one line per person who stops):**

| # | Time | Water source (tap/borewell/tanker) | Strip reading (ppm) | Main question/objection (their words) | Scanned QR? | Paid pre-order? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Summary at the end:** people passing (rough count) · stopped · tested · scanned · **paid pre-orders** · refunds within 7 days · total cost · cost per paid pre-order · top 3 objections verbatim.
