# Mrudu images: shot list and stock candidates

Image rule (CEO decision): no images from other websites. Only (a) our own photos, (b) free-licence stock for mood only, (c) our own illustrations labelled "Illustration". Nothing has been downloaded.

## (a) Shot list: one phone, one hour, one real unit

Setup: daylight by a window, phone on a tripod or propped, gridlines on, beauty/AI enhance **off**, no filters. Backdrop: a plain white or pale grey sheet (reads as Limestone). Shoot 4:5 photos and 9:16 video. Edits limited to crop and exposure. Save originals to `marketing/evidence/` named `YYYY-MM-DD_area_shot_litres.ext`.

| # | Shot | Time | Used for | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unit alone on backdrop: side-on and three-quarter, then box + cartridge + strips flat lay | 10 min | Landing hero, end card (replaces `product-render.svg`) | None |
| 2 | Unit fitted on a real shower arm with a shower head: wide, then close | 10 min | Landing "fits" section, angle B | Fit test done |
| 3 | Fitting it, one continuous take, start to finish | 5 min | Landing, supports "no plumber needed" | Fit test done |
| 4 | Macro of real white scale on a tap and a shower head (our flat or written owner OK) | 10 min | Angle D | No people, no door numbers, no visible brand logos |
| 5 | Unused strip next to the colour chart on the tube | 5 min | Landing "check it yourself" | None |
| 6 | Strip test: glass from bare arm, dip, hold to chart; fit unit, second glass, dip, compare. One take | 10 min | Angle A | **Only after the cartridge-capacity test**, filmed after a realistic volume has passed; note litres in file name |
| 7 | Shake test: two identical bottles, marked water line, 3 drops same soap, 10 shakes, hold still | 10 min | Angle C | **Same gate as 6**; drop angle C if no clear difference |
Never shoot: people's skin, hair or faces; before/after of bodies; a "cleaned" shower head presented as a result; competitors' logos.
