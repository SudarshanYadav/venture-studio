CPO Go/No-Go calculator

Smoke test: Meta ads to a refundable pre-order page. Compares the cost to get one pre-order (CPO) against the most we can afford to pay for a customer. Every input is an assumption — change it and the verdict updates.

Verdict: CPO ≤ 70% of max affordable cost = GO · 70–100% = ITERATE · above 100% = KILL

How it's calculated. Profit per order = price (minus GST if included) − product cost − shipping/packaging/payment fees − returns allowance (a % of price). Max affordable cost per customer = first-order profit + repeat profit × share counted. GO threshold = 70% of that, leaving headroom because ad costs rise as spend scales.

Test CPO = cost per click ÷ landing-page conversion rate. When spend and paid pre-orders are entered, the actual figure replaces the estimate.

Confidence. With few orders the measured CPO is noisy. The range shown is a 95% interval on the order count (Poisson). At 25 orders the true CPO could be about 0.7× to 1.5× what you measured, so treat a verdict as solid only when the whole range sits on one side of a threshold.

Payback counts all expected repeat profit (not just the share counted) and assumes steady repeat orders with no drop-off, so treat it as a best case.
