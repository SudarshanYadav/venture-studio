Softening Cartridge Capacity
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The chart below expresses the theoretical performance capacity of our water softening cartridges in four sizes. It assumes the standard 32,000 grain per cubic foot capacity of new cation softening resin.
Softening cartridges can be regenerated, partially but not perfectly, by soaking them in a salt water solution.
Go here for details. Figures for regenerated resin are lower.
The chart is for information only and is not a guarantee of performance. Actual performance can be influenced by a number of variables.
  Cartridge Size   Gallons of 7 Grains Per Gallon Water Treated   Gallons of 15 Grains Per Gallon Water Treated   Gallons of 40 Grains Per Gallon Water Treated
  ---------------- ---------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------
  2.5" X 9.75"     105                                            45                                              15
  2.5" X 20"       255                                            120                                             45
  4.25" X 10"      263                                            128                                             45
  4.25" X 20"      615                                            285                                             105
Garden Hose Softener Capacity
Below is a summary of performance capacity for a Garden Hose Water Softener. The capacity is greater than any of our standard softening cartridges, and the regeneration process is simplified.
  Tank Size   Gallons of 7 Grains Per Gallon Water Treated   Gallons of 15 Grains Per Gallon Water Treated   Gallons of 40 Grains Per Gallon Water Treated
  ----------- ---------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------
  8" X 18"    1400                                           650                                             250
Deductions are made using the standard rules of cylinder volume: the volume of a cylinder equals the (area of the base)*height = (πr²)h. 1 cubic inches = 0.000578703704 cubic feet.
