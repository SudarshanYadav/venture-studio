FAQs
Why does it not reduce TDS? +
TDS only shows that something is dissolved in water—it doesn’t indicate if it’s good or bad. CLEO Tap Filters convert hard water salts into harmless forms so they don’t stick to your hair, skin, or surfaces, and are simply washed away. Your water stays healthier without changing the TDS..
When can I start noticing a difference? +
You’ll notice an immediate difference in lathering and the feel of the water. Visible results on hair and skin usually take about 6 weeks. You can also try the simple ‘lather test’ to check the product’s effectiveness with your water.
Is it easy to install? +
Installation takes just a few minutes and you can follow the step-by-step video guide.
How can I get installation support? +
Contact our Customer Care Team at 1800-121-0599 or chat with our team on WhatsApp for installation support.
