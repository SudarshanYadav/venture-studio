Hard water leaves a mark you can see.

White crust around taps and the shower head. Chalky spots on glass and tiles. Soap and shampoo that take more to lather. These come from calcium and magnesium in the water, and they are what a hardness strip measures.

A chlorine shower filter

Usually carbon or KDF media. Built to reduce chlorine, which city supply is treated with. Borewell water usually reaches your tank without city chlorination, so a chlorine filter may do little about scale.

Mrudu, a resin softener

Ion-exchange resin swaps calcium and magnesium for sodium as water passes through. That is the same principle a whole-house softener uses, sized for one shower.

Check it yourself in two minutes.

Test strips come in the box. You do not have to take our word for it.

  1. Dip a strip in water straight from your tap and note the colour.
  2. Fit Mrudu between your shower arm and shower head, then run the shower for a few seconds.
  3. Dip a second strip in the shower water and compare both against the colour chart on the pack.
